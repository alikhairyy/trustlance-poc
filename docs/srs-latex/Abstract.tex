\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This document is the software requirements specification (SRS) for Trustlance, a freelance platform in which each milestone payment is locked in a smart contract. When a freelancer submits work, an explainable machine-learning model scores the wallet that will receive the payment. A high score holds the payment for at most seven days so that a human arbiter can review it, and the freelancer can appeal. The AI cannot move money. The document defines 20 functional and 12 non-functional requirements, the use case, activity, entity-relationship and class diagrams, and the constraints and assumptions behind the design. It also describes the first part of the implementation: a web application for the three roles, a REST API, a database schema, and the escrow contract and risk engine from the proof of concept, all connected. Thirteen functional requirements are implemented and covered by 48 automated tests, two work with a named development stand-in, two are stubs and three are planned.
