\chapter{System Scope and Objectives}
\label{ch:scope}

\section{Main objective}
Design and build a freelance payment system that secures milestone payments with a smart contract and an explainable risk score, with a human arbiter making the final decision on any payment that is held or disputed.

\section{Specific objectives}
\begin{enumerate}
\item Implement milestone escrow in a smart contract with separate roles for the client, freelancer, arbiter and risk oracle.
\item Build a risk engine that scores the payout wallet from 0 to 1 and explains the score in plain language.
\item Train and benchmark the model on real labelled data, and measure what choosing an explainable model costs in accuracy.
\item Make sure the AI can only hold a payment for human review, can never move money, and that a hold ends after a fixed window.
\item Provide an on-chain appeal and a tamper-evident log of every automated decision.
\item Give each role a web application, then evaluate the system with a survey and a small pilot.
\end{enumerate}
Objectives 1 to 5 were met by the proof of concept in Milestone~1. Objective 6 is the main work of this milestone and the next.

\section{In scope}
\begin{itemize}
\item A web application for clients, freelancers and arbiters.
\item A REST API and a private database for accounts, jobs, proposals and milestones.
\item The milestone escrow smart contract, with arbiter, appeal and pause.
\item The explainable wallet risk engine, its benchmark and its audit log.
\item A survey and a small pilot on a public test network.
\end{itemize}

\section{Out of scope}
\begin{itemize}
\item Real money on the Ethereum main network before an independent security audit.
\item Matching freelancers to jobs, or recommending them.
\item Converting between ETH and local currency.
\item Legal arbitration across countries.
\end{itemize}
